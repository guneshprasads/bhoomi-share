How it works

Two separate things happen here, and they do not depend on each other. Read whichever one brought you.

Funding a season

One crop, one named parcel, one season. When it is sold, the proceeds are split the way the agreement says.

  1. 01

    A grower posts a plan

    Parcel and survey number, crop, sowing window, a costed input budget, the yield they expect, and the split they want.

  2. 02

    You read it and register interest

    With an amount. Nothing is paid and nothing is binding — it tells the grower whether the plan is fundable.

  3. 03

    The season runs

    Sowing to harvest. The grower posts what was spent and what went in the ground, against the plan you read.

  4. 04

    The harvest sells and proceeds split

    Mandi or contract sale, receipts shared, split as signed. Costs come out first; what is left is divided.

What you are funding, and what you are not

  • You are funding the inputs and labour of one crop cycle on one parcel.
  • You are not buying land, a share of a company, or a financial product with a stated return.
  • There is no guaranteed return. A season that fails splits the same way a good one does.
  • Between registering interest and any money moving there is a partnership deed, a capped pool and counsel's sign-off. Why.

Leasing land

An owner who is not farming this year, a farmer who wants more land, and a fixed-term licence between them.

  1. 01

    List or search

    Acreage, survey number, water source and hours, soil, road access, what was last grown. Farmers search by district and by what they can irrigate.

  2. 02

    Agree on terms

    Rent per acre per season, or a share of the crop. Who pays for borewell power, who repairs the bunds, who keeps the residue, what happens if the rain fails.

  3. 03

    Sign a proper licence

    A fixed-term licence to cultivate, drafted for the state the land sits in, signed and witnessed. Deliberately not an open-ended tenancy.

  4. 04

    Renew or move on

    At the end of the term the land comes back, with a record of how it was farmed. Renew if it went well; list it again if it did not.

What this site does and does not do

  • It puts the parcel in front of farmers and passes on your phone number when one writes to you.
  • It does not hold deposits, collect rent, or execute the licence. You sign on paper, with your own witnesses.
  • It does not verify title. Ask for the 7/12 or the RTC and read it, the way you would anyway.

What it costs

Nothing, during the pilot. We are not charging investors, growers, owners or farmers while we are still proving this on our own 15 acres. When there is a fee it will be stated on this page before it applies to anyone.

Where it runs

The pilot is on our family's 15 acres. We open other districts one at a time, after a full season of real numbers — including the parts that went badly. Tell us your district and you will hear when it opens.
