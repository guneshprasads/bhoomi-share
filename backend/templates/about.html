The pilot

We farm 15 acres in Belagavi district as a family. Every model on this site runs there first: our own capital in the investor seat, our own soil, a full season of numbers, and one parcel licensed out on the agreement we drafted. We want those numbers, including the parts that went badly, before we take money or land from anyone else.

That is why the site is pre-launch, why no money moves through it, and why we open one district at a time.

What we believe

  • Show the failed season.Every calculator, story and model page shows what happens when the crop fails. A site that only shows the good case is selling something.
  • Paper, not handshakes.Most land arrangements already happen by word of mouth. That works until it does not. Short, fixed-term agreements protect both sides.
  • Small on purpose.Pools are capped and kept small. We would rather grow slowly than fall foul of the rules on collective investment.
  • People decide.The site puts plans and parcels in front of people. It does not hold money, verify title or sign for anyone.
  • Two languages, equally.Farming in Karnataka happens in Kannada. Every page is available in English and Kannada.
