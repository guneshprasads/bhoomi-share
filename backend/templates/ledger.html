Every plan gets an account that has to add up.

Import a ledger, or try the example farm. Each plan is checked against simple balance rules, scored for trust, and shown as money in and money out. Not reported is never read as zero.

Money in → money out, per plan

Left: what the season brought in. Right: where it went. Hatched is money the file cannot account for. Plans with no sold season show what the plan expects instead.

Does each account add up?

Four rules, each allowed ±5%: the budget lines match the stated budget, spending was logged and stayed near budget, a sold season's receipts equal what was paid out, and there are enough past seasons to judge the spread.

Same land, very different seasons

Yield per unit for each past season (bars), against what the plan expects and a district benchmark (assumed). The reconciled estimate weights each source by how precise it is.

What the inputs cost

Budget lines grouped by category. Move the sliders to see what a price change does to every plan's budget. Unit costs here are planning values: replace them with real quotes.

How the import reads a file

One CSV, five columns. The record column decides where each number goes, so any ledger can be read, not just this one.

recordwhat it becomes
metaA fact about the plan: name, district, kind, area, investor_pct, stated_budget, expected_yield, expected_price
costA budget line: label is the item, value the rupees
spentWhat the log says was spent
historyOne past season: season, label yield or price, value
saleReceipts for a sold season

        
Needs a human: anything it cannot read is listed, not guessed. A spend of - stays not reported; a cost of abc is skipped and named.
