The problem

Three things rarely sit together, and nobody can see the bad case.

1

Land, money and skill are split up

A parcel needs land, working capital and someone who can farm it. Those rarely sit with one person, so good land sits idle and good farmers sit short of money.

2

Deals run on a handshake

Most land arrangements already happen by word of mouth, on no paper, because writing anything down can build tenancy rights. It works until it does not.

3

Nobody sees the bad case

A budget that does not add up, a season nobody logged, a yield claimed with no history. A funder is asked to trust numbers nobody has checked.

What it costs

Free while we prove it.

We are not charging funders, growers, owners or farmers during the pilot. We take no cut of any plan’s proceeds, and no money passes through the site.

If there is ever a fee, it will be decided with counsel, stated on the how-it-works page before it applies to anyone, and never buried in a split. We would rather not have a business model than one we cannot explain.

What is data, what is model, what is assumed

  • Data: the ledger’s budget lines, logs and past seasons. The example is invented and says so.
  • Model: a seeded simulation of yield and price, with a failed-season event.
  • Assumed: yield spread, price swing, failure chance, every fix’s cost and effect. All editable on the page.

Risk outputs are simulations from stated assumptions, not forecasts.
